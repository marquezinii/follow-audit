How to see who unfollowed you on Instagram (and why the first check is only a baseline)

Instagram doesn't notify you when someone unfollows you, so you need two snapshots to compare. Run the free FollowAudit script on instagram.com once to save your follower list in your browser, then run it again later. The second audit lists everyone who left since the first. How to see who unfollowed you on Instagram, step by step

Run FollowAudit once to save a baseline of your followers, then run it again a day, a week or a month later from the same browser and the same account. The second audit compares the two lists and opens an “Unfollowed you” view with every account that left since the earlier audit.

  1. Open FollowAudit on InstagramCopy the script on the FollowAudit page, open instagram.com on a computer while logged in, press F12 (Cmd + Option + J on a Mac), paste it into the Console tab and press Enter.
  2. Run your first auditSelect Start audit. When it finishes, the panel says “Baseline saved”: your follower list is now stored in this browser, and there is nothing to compare it with yet.
  3. Wait, then audit againCome back later in the same browser, logged in to the same account, paste the script again and start a new audit. How long you wait is up to you.
  4. Open “Unfollowed you”This view lists followers from the previous audit who no longer follow you, with the date of that audit. A tag on each account shows whether you still follow it.

How does FollowAudit know who unfollowed you?

After every audit, FollowAudit saves the ID, username and name of each follower in your browser's local storage, kept separately for each account. The next audit compares the fresh list with that saved one. Anyone who was there before and is missing now appears in “Unfollowed you”. Nothing is sent to a server.

Why is the list empty after my first audit?

Because there is nothing to compare with yet. The first audit only creates the baseline, and the panel tells you so: “Baseline saved. Your next audit will reveal who unfollowed you.” The “Unfollowed you” view shows up from the second audit on.

Does it compare with my first audit or the last one?

The last one. Every audit replaces the saved list, so the view always shows who left since the previous audit. If you want a weekly picture, audit once a week. Two audits an hour apart will usually show nobody, which is the right answer.

Does it work on another computer or browser?

No. The saved list lives in one browser profile on one computer. A different browser, a private window or cleared site data starts again from a new baseline. You can also delete it yourself at any time in Settings with Clear audit history.

Can I see who unfollowed me before I started?

No. FollowAudit only knows the follower lists it has seen, and Instagram doesn't show a history of who stopped following you. The record starts with your first audit.

Does this work on TikTok?

Yes, read-only. Each time you import a TikTok data export, FollowAudit compares its follower list with the previous export you imported in the same browser. TikTok exports can lag behind recent changes, so treat the result as approximate.

What the “Unfollowed you” list can't tell you

It shows who is missing from your followers, not why. An account that was deactivated or deleted, blocked you, or was removed by Instagram disappears from your list the same way as one that tapped Unfollow. Very large accounts, with roughly 30,000 followers or more, are not tracked at all.

Did they unfollow me, or is the account gone?

FollowAudit compares account IDs, so it can't tell the difference. Open the profile before you read anything into it. If Instagram says the page isn't available, the account may have been deactivated or deleted, or the person may have blocked you.

What if someone changed their username?

That doesn't count as an unfollow. Accounts are matched by their numeric ID, which stays the same when a username changes, so a rename never shows up as someone leaving. The list shows the username saved at the previous audit.

Why isn't my large account tracked?

The saved list has to fit in the browser's local storage. Follower lists of roughly 30,000 accounts or more are too big to store, so FollowAudit skips saving them and the “Unfollowed you” view never appears. The rest of the audit works as usual.

Do followers I removed myself count?

If you remove followers with FollowAudit and then refresh the audit in the same session, those accounts are left out, because they didn't unfollow you. Followers you removed in Instagram's own app, or in an earlier session, will show up.

Can I unfollow them from that list?

Not from that view, which is read-only. If you still follow someone who left, they also appear in “Don’t follow you back”, where you can select them. Before you do, read how Instagram's unfollow limits work.

Is it safe? Risks to know before you run it

Tracking unfollows only reads your lists and changes nothing on your account, but it still uses private Instagram web endpoints that can change without notice, and Instagram's terms restrict automated access. Nobody can promise your account will never be limited or banned. Use it at your own risk.

  • Private endpointsFollowAudit relies on private Instagram web endpoints, not an official API. They can change or stop working at any time, and the script may break without warning.
  • Instagram's termsInstagram's terms of use restrict automated access to the service. Running any script against your account, this one included, is a risk you take.
  • Don't audit every few minutesEach audit loads both full lists page by page. On a big account that is a lot of requests, and checking constantly tells you nothing new. Once a day or once a week is enough.
  • The saved list stays on this computerYour follower list is stored in this browser profile so the next audit can compare. Anyone who uses the same profile could read it. Clear it in Settings when you no longer need it.
  • The code runs in your sessionA console script can do anything your logged-in tab can do. Only paste code you trust, and read it first. FollowAudit is MIT-licensed and open on GitHub. Never paste a script a stranger sends you.
