Privacy

Private by design.
Deliberate by default.

Your lists and imported files stay in your browser. Follow Audit has no backend, no analytics, and never uploads your account data.

Safety principles

  • Review before action
  • Protected accounts stay excluded
  • Read-only TikTok audit
  • Explicit confirmation every time

Instagram audits use private web endpoints that can change without notice. TikTok audits use only the JSON files you choose and never automate account changes.
